# Generic 40t Articulated Dump Truck — Operator's Manual

Synthetic manual written for the ReBase demo. Generic machine, no brand. Not an OEM document.

## 1 Safety

### 1.1 General safety rules
Always wear your seatbelt. Keep people out of the articulation zone. Never raise the body on a slope, near overhead power lines or in strong wind. Never travel with the body raised.

### 1.2 Haul roads
Follow the site speed limits and keep left or right as the site rules say. Keep a safe distance from the vehicle in front: at least 50 m on haul roads. Use lower gears and the retarder on downhill ramps.

## 2 Cab and controls

### 2.1 Cab layout
The steering wheel, gear selector, service brake and accelerator are in front of the seat. The body hoist lever and retarder lever are on the right. The monitor panel shows speed, gear, load and warnings.

### 2.2 Retarder
The retarder slows the truck without using the service brakes. Use it on every downhill section to keep speed under control and save the brakes.

## 3 Starting and stopping

### 3.1 Starting the engine
Complete the pre-start checklist. Put the gear selector in neutral and apply the parking brake. Fasten your seatbelt, sound the horn and start the engine.

### 3.2 Stopping
Park on level ground away from the haul road, lower the body, select neutral, apply the parking brake and idle for 3 minutes before stopping.

## 4 Operating

### 4.1 Being loaded
Stop where the loader operator signals, apply the parking brake and stay in the cab. Watch the load indicator.

### 4.2 Driving loaded
Drive at a steady speed. Do not exceed 15 km/h when the truck is more than 80 percent loaded on rough ground. Slow down before bends: a loaded articulated truck can tip if it turns fast.

### 4.3 Tipping the load
Stop on firm, level ground at the tipping point with the truck straight. Apply the parking brake. Raise the body slowly with the hoist lever. Drive forward a few metres only after the load has slid out, then lower the body fully before driving on.

### 4.4 Operating modes
The truck has an automatic gear mode for normal hauling and a manual gear hold for steep ramps. To hold a gear on a ramp, press the gear-hold button on the gear selector before starting down the ramp.

## 5 Warning lights and alarms

### 5.1 Body-up warning
The body-up warning shows while the body is not fully lowered. Never drive with this warning on.

### 5.2 Overload warning
The load indicator warns above 90 percent of the rated load and shows an overload above 100 percent. Ask the loader to remove material; an overloaded truck brakes poorly.

### 5.3 Brake and retarder temperature
If the brake or retarder temperature warning comes on, reduce speed, use a lower gear and stop on a safe level area to let the brakes cool.

## 6 Emergency procedures

### 6.1 Brake failure
Use the retarder and shift down to slow the truck. Steer towards an uphill escape lane or a safety berm. Apply the parking brake when the speed is low. Warn others by horn and radio.
