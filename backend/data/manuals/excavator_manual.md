# Generic 20t Hydraulic Excavator — Operator's Manual

Synthetic manual written for the ReBase demo. Generic machine, no brand. Not an OEM document.

## 1 Safety

### 1.1 General safety rules
Always wear your seatbelt while the engine is running. Use three points of contact when climbing on or off the machine. Never let anyone ride on the machine outside the cab. Do not operate the excavator if you are tired, unwell or under the influence of alcohol or medication that causes drowsiness. Report every defect before starting work.

### 1.2 Working near power lines
Keep every part of the machine, including the boom at full reach and the load, at least 6 m from overhead power lines. If you are unsure of the voltage, treat the line as live and keep a larger distance. Use a spotter whenever the boom may come close to a line. If the machine touches a live line, stay in the cab and warn others to keep away until the power is switched off.

### 1.3 Ground crew and swing area
Keep at least 10 m between the machine and the ground crew unless a signaller has made eye contact with you and waved them in. The counterweight swings behind the machine: check the swing area is clear before every swing. Never swing the bucket over people or vehicles. Stop immediately if the proximity warning sounds.

## 2 Cab and controls

### 2.1 Cab layout
The left joystick and right joystick control the working equipment. The two travel levers with foot pedals in front of the seat control the tracks. The monitor panel is on the right-hand console. The hydraulic lock lever is on the left side of the seat. The horn button is on top of the right joystick.

### 2.2 Joystick control pattern (ISO)
This machine uses the ISO control pattern. Left joystick: forward and back moves the arm out and in; left and right swings the upper structure left and right. Right joystick: forward and back lowers and raises the boom; left and right curls the bucket in and dumps it out. Move the joysticks smoothly; sudden movements shake the machine and can spill the load.

### 2.3 Hydraulic lock lever
Pull the hydraulic lock lever up to lock all hydraulic functions before you leave the seat, before you let anyone approach the machine, and when you start the engine. Push it down to unlock. With the lever up, the joysticks and travel levers do nothing.

### 2.4 Monitor panel
The monitor panel shows engine speed, hydraulic oil temperature, coolant temperature, fuel level, the selected working mode and warning messages. The mode button (the "M" button) selects the working mode. The auto-idle button switches auto-idle on or off.

## 3 Starting and stopping

### 3.1 Before starting
Complete the pre-start checklist on the tablet. Check the area around the machine is clear. Adjust the seat and mirrors. Fasten your seatbelt. Make sure the hydraulic lock lever is up and all levers are in neutral.

### 3.2 Starting the engine
Turn the key to ON and wait for the monitor self-check to finish. Sound the horn to warn people nearby. Turn the key to START; release it as soon as the engine starts. Do not crank for more than 15 seconds; wait 30 seconds before trying again.

### 3.3 Warm-up
Run the engine at low speed for 5 minutes. Slowly operate each hydraulic function a few times to warm the hydraulic oil. In cold weather, warm up until the hydraulic oil temperature reaches at least 20 °C before heavy digging.

### 3.4 Stopping the engine
Park on firm, level ground. Lower the bucket flat to the ground. Let the engine idle for 3 to 5 minutes to cool the turbocharger. Turn the key to OFF, pull the hydraulic lock lever up and remove the key.

## 4 Operating

### 4.1 Travel
Before travelling, check whether the travel motors are at the front or the rear: if they are at the front, the travel levers work in reverse. Travel with the bucket about 40 cm above the ground. The travel alarm sounds automatically. Use low travel speed on rough or sloping ground.

### 4.2 Operating modes
The excavator has four working modes. Power mode (P) gives maximum engine speed and hydraulic flow for heavy digging and fast cycles. Economy mode (E) lowers engine speed to save fuel for normal digging and loading. Lifting mode (L) gives slow, precise movements for lifting objects. Breaker mode (B) sets the hydraulic flow for a hydraulic breaker attachment.
To switch to power mode: put both joysticks in neutral, press the mode button (M) on the monitor panel until "P" is shown, then confirm. The mode changes only when the joysticks are in neutral. Use power mode only when you need it; economy mode uses about 10 to 15 percent less fuel.

### 4.3 Auto-idle
With auto-idle on, the engine drops to idle about 4 seconds after all levers return to neutral and speeds up again when you move a lever. Keep auto-idle on to save fuel. If the machine will be idle for more than 5 minutes, stop the engine: long idling wastes fuel and loads the engine.

### 4.4 Digging and trenching
Dig with the arm pulling in towards the machine; this is the most efficient movement. Keep the tracks at right angles to the trench when possible. Do not dig under the machine or under the tracks. Keep the trench edge at least 1 m from the tracks in soft ground. Do not use the swing to push material sideways or to hit rock.

### 4.5 Lifting objects
Select lifting mode (L) before lifting. Use only the approved lifting point on the bucket linkage. Check the lift chart on the cab window for the maximum load at your radius. Never lift over people. Keep the load as low as possible and swing slowly.

### 4.6 Working on slopes
Do not work on slopes steeper than 15 degrees. Travel straight up or down a slope, never across it. Keep the bucket low and towards the uphill side. Soft ground after rain can collapse under the tracks: check the ground before moving onto it.

## 5 Warning lights and alarms

### 5.1 Hydraulic oil temperature warning
The hydraulic oil temperature is normally between 50 and 80 °C. Above 95 °C the warning light comes on: reduce the load, switch to economy mode and let the machine run at low idle to cool down. Above 105 °C stop work, idle the engine for 5 minutes, then stop it and report the fault. Check for a blocked oil cooler, low oil level or a slipping fan belt.

### 5.2 Engine coolant temperature warning
If the coolant temperature warning comes on, stop work and let the engine idle. Do not stop the engine immediately and do not open the radiator cap while hot. If the warning stays on, stop the engine and report it.

### 5.3 Low fuel warning
The low fuel warning comes on when about 10 percent fuel is left. Refuel at the end of the shift to stop condensation forming in the tank. Never let the tank run dry: the fuel system then needs bleeding.

### 5.4 Seatbelt reminder
The seatbelt reminder sounds and the tablet shows a warning if the seatbelt is not fastened while the engine is running. Fasten the seatbelt before you move the machine.

### 5.5 Proximity warning
The proximity warning sounds when a person or obstacle is closer than 5 m and becomes critical closer than 2 m. Stop all movement, find what triggered it and wait until the area is clear.

## 6 Daily maintenance

### 6.1 Walk-around inspection
Walk around the machine before every shift. Look for leaks, damaged hoses, cracks in the boom and arm, loose or missing pins and retainers, worn bucket teeth and damaged tracks. Record the results on the tablet checklist.

### 6.2 Greasing
Grease the boom, arm and bucket pins every 10 hours, and every 50 hours for the swing bearing. Grease more often when working in water or mud. Lower the bucket to the ground before greasing.

### 6.3 Fluid levels
Check engine oil and coolant with the machine level and the engine stopped. Check hydraulic oil with the arm and bucket fully retracted and the bucket on the ground. Top up only with the specified fluids.

## 7 Troubleshooting

### 7.1 Hydraulics are slow or weak
Check the working mode: economy and lifting modes are slower by design. Check the hydraulic oil level and temperature; cold oil makes the machine slow until it warms up. If the hydraulics are still slow, report it.

### 7.2 Engine does not start
Check the hydraulic lock lever is up: the engine will not start with the lever down. Check the fuel level and the battery isolator switch. Do not crank for more than 15 seconds at a time.

## 8 Emergency procedures

### 8.1 Emergency engine stop
Turn the key to OFF. If the engine does not stop, press the emergency stop button on the right console. Pull the hydraulic lock lever up.

### 8.2 Lowering the boom with the engine stopped
If the engine stops with the bucket raised, keep everyone clear. Turn the key to ON without starting, pull the lock lever down and move the right joystick slowly forward: the boom lowers under its own weight using stored pressure. Then lock the lever and report the fault.

### 8.3 Fire
Stop the engine, turn off the battery isolator and leave the cab. Use the fire extinguisher only if it is safe; otherwise move away and raise the alarm.
