# Generic 3.5 m³ Wheel Loader — Operator's Manual

Synthetic manual written for the ReBase demo. Generic machine, no brand. Not an OEM document.

## 1 Safety

### 1.1 General safety rules
Always wear your seatbelt while the engine is running. Use three points of contact on the steps. Never let anyone ride on the machine outside the cab. Keep people out of the articulation zone between the front and rear frames: it closes when the loader steers.

### 1.2 Visibility and reversing
Loaders spend much of their time reversing. Check the mirrors and rear camera before every reverse. The reverse alarm must work. If you cannot see behind the machine, use a spotter.

## 2 Cab and controls

### 2.1 Cab layout
The steering wheel and the direction lever (forward, neutral, reverse) are in front of the seat. The loader joystick on the right console controls the lift arms and the bucket. The parking brake switch and the monitor panel are on the right-hand console.

### 2.2 Loader joystick
Pull the joystick back to raise the lift arms and push it forward to lower them. Move it left to curl the bucket back and right to dump. Push it fully forward into the detent to put the lift arms in float mode, which lets the bucket follow the ground.

### 2.3 Articulation lock
The articulation lock bar stops the machine from steering during transport and maintenance. Remove it and store it in its holder before you drive; the machine cannot steer while it is fitted.

## 3 Starting and stopping

### 3.1 Starting the engine
Complete the pre-start checklist. Put the direction lever in neutral and apply the parking brake. Fasten your seatbelt, sound the horn and turn the key to START.

### 3.2 Stopping the engine
Park on level ground, lower the bucket flat to the ground, put the direction lever in neutral and apply the parking brake. Idle for 3 minutes before stopping the engine.

## 4 Operating

### 4.1 Loading from a stockpile
Approach the pile straight with the bucket flat on the ground. Drive into the pile in low gear, then curl the bucket back while raising the lift arms slowly. Do not spin the wheels: it wears the tyres quickly.

### 4.2 Operating modes
The loader has two drive modes. Power mode gives full engine power for hard digging and climbing ramps. Economy mode limits engine speed to save fuel during normal loading. To switch mode, stop the machine or keep the direction lever in neutral and press the mode switch on the right console. The selected mode is shown on the monitor panel.

### 4.3 Travelling with a load
Carry the bucket low, about 40 cm above the ground, with the bucket curled back. A raised bucket makes the loader unstable and blocks your view. Slow down before turns: articulated machines can tip when turning fast with a load. Keep travel speed below 10 km/h with a full bucket.

### 4.4 Loading trucks
Approach the truck at a right angle. Raise the bucket only when you are close to the truck and moving slowly. Dump the load gently in the centre of the truck body. Never load over the truck cab while the driver is inside.

## 5 Warning lights and alarms

### 5.1 Hydraulic and transmission temperature
If the hydraulic or transmission oil temperature warning comes on (above 95 °C), reduce the load and let the machine idle to cool down. Above 105 °C stop work and report the fault.

### 5.2 Brake pressure warning
If the brake pressure warning comes on, stop the machine as soon as it is safe, apply the parking brake and do not continue until the fault is repaired.

### 5.3 Overload
The load indicator warns when the bucket load is above 90 percent of the rated load; above 100 percent the machine is overloaded. Dump part of the load.

## 6 Daily maintenance

### 6.1 Tyres
Check tyres for cuts, bulges and correct pressure before every shift. Do not stand in front of a tyre while inflating it. Report any damage that shows the cords.

### 6.2 Greasing
Grease the lift arm, bucket and articulation pins every 10 hours.

## 7 Emergency procedures

### 7.1 Emergency stop and steering failure
If the steering fails, stop the machine with the service brake and apply the parking brake. Lower the bucket to the ground. The emergency steering pump gives limited steering for a short time only.
