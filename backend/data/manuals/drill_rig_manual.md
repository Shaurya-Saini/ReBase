# Generic Crawler Drill Rig — Operator's Manual

Synthetic manual written for the ReBase demo. Generic machine, no brand. Not an OEM document.

## 1 Safety

### 1.1 General safety rules
Wear your seatbelt when tramming. Wear hearing protection, safety glasses and a dust mask when outside the cab during drilling. Never reach into the rotation area while the drill is running. Keep people at least 5 m from the drill string.

### 1.2 Bench edges
Keep the tracks at least 3 m from the bench edge, or further if the ground is broken. Never tram parallel to the edge with the mast raised. Mark the edge with a berm or cones.

## 2 Cab and controls

### 2.1 Cab layout
The tramming levers are in front of the seat. The drilling control panel on the right console has the rotation, feed and percussion controls. Emergency stop buttons are in the cab and on the drill deck.

### 2.2 Drilling controls
The rotation control turns the drill string. The feed control pushes the drill string down or pulls it up. Percussion is switched on only after collaring the hole. The automatic rod changer adds and removes rods.

## 3 Starting and stopping

### 3.1 Starting the engine
Complete the pre-start checklist. Check the emergency stop buttons. Put all controls in neutral, fasten the seatbelt and start the engine. Let the compressor warm up for 5 minutes before drilling.

### 3.2 Stopping
Pull the drill string out of the hole, lower the mast to the transport position, idle for 3 minutes and stop the engine.

## 4 Operating

### 4.1 Setting up over a hole
Tram to the marked hole position with the mast lowered. Level the rig using the jacks. Raise the mast and align it with the planned angle. Check the centralizer holds the rod straight.

### 4.2 Collaring and drilling
Start the hole with low feed pressure and low percussion (collaring) until the bit is about 30 cm into the rock, then switch to full drilling. Watch the rotation pressure: a sudden rise means the bit is getting stuck.

### 4.3 Drilling modes
The rig has a soft-rock mode with lower percussion pressure and a hard-rock mode with full percussion. Select the mode on the drilling control panel before collaring; the mode cannot be changed while percussion is on.

### 4.4 Dust control
Keep the dust collector running during drilling. If dust escapes around the collar, check the collector hoses and the suction hood.

## 5 Warning lights and alarms

### 5.1 Hydraulic oil temperature
Above 95 °C reduce percussion and let the rig cool at idle. Above 105 °C stop drilling and report the fault.

### 5.2 Compressor temperature
If the compressor temperature warning comes on, stop drilling and let the compressor run unloaded to cool down.

## 6 Emergency procedures

### 6.1 Stuck drill string
Stop percussion. Reverse rotation slowly while pulling back with low feed. Do not use full force: the rods can break. If the string stays stuck, stop and report it.

### 6.2 Emergency stop
Press any emergency stop button to stop the engine and all functions immediately.
